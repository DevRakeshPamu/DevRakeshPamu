About me
I build apps people love.

I've spent 7+ years building Android and Flutter apps — and I genuinely enjoy the work. Not just the coding part, but the problem-solving. A client comes with an idea and I help them figure out what actually makes sense to build, how to build it right, and how to make sure users actually like using it.

Most of my work has been in media and streaming — I built Tale'O'Meter, a music streaming app with 50K+ users and 98.79% crash-free rate, an OTT platform for a regional content provider, and a Doctor Plaza healthcare app with Twilio video calling and Razorpay payments.

I also built a Remote Assist AR app using WebRTC and OpenGL — real-time peer-to-peer communication between Android and iOS for remote technical assistance. That's not something most Android developers have done.

I care about clean architecture, proper testing, and documentation that makes sense to the next developer. I'll tell you if something doesn't make sense instead of just building it wrong.
